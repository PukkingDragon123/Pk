BITE DOWN
A PUSH-YOUR-LUCK DENTAL ROGUELIKE
🐊 VERSION 1.0

You're a back-bayou dentist with a very bad idea: press a swamp gator's teeth one at a time and hope the jaw doesn't SNAP. Every safe tooth pumps your TEETH × MULT score… but somewhere in that maw is a snapper. Bank your bite, or push your luck. 🦷

🕹️ How to play

  • Press teeth — each safe one adds TEETH and grows your MULT chain.
  • Bank the bite to lock in TEETH × MULT — or dive deeper for more.
  • Hit the hidden snap tooth and you lose the whole unbanked bite. 💥
  • Spend at the Everglades Trading Post on special teeth, tools & snack packs.

✨ Features

  • 🦷 20+ special teeth & 45+ charm badges for wild score combos
  • 🔧 10 dentist tools & snack-pack openings at the workbench
  • 🐊 20+ gator variants and 17 boss gators with nasty gimmicks
  • 🗺️ Choose your path across a colorful, branching swamp trail
  • 🎖️ Park rangers to unlock, a Ranger Exchange & permanent quests
  • 🎵 Chiptune soundtrack, juicy pixel FX and x-ray tooth peeks

🎮 Controls

Mouse or touch — click teeth, drag cards to use or sell them, and click anything for full info. Press ESC to pause. Plays right in your browser, no download. 🌙
